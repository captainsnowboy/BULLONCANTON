A bull in a suit sprints along the ground, rams a thick, real-looking brick wall,
bounces off, comes back and rams again. Each hit adds cracks. The WALL ST. street sign
above the wall shakes and gets knocked loose. On the final hit the wall bursts. The wall
must read as a solid 3D object (front face, a side face with thickness, a stone top cap,
a ground shadow), drawn in the flat, thick-outline ink style of the bull art. NO grid of
square tiles or dots: it must look like staggered brickwork.

1. ENGINE (one engine, two inputs)
- Constants at the top of the file: HOLD_SECONDS = 5; RAM_THRESHOLDS = [0.62, 0.78,
  0.90]; BREACH = 1; BRICK_CAP = 60.
- Inputs only set `target` (0 to 1). Mobile (under 768px): holding the button raises
  target by 1/HOLD_SECONDS per second; releasing lowers it by 0.12 per second but never
  below the last ram threshold already reached. Desktop (768px and up): ScrollTrigger
  pins the section (about +=350% scroll) and maps scroll to target.
- `shown` follows `target`. It is blocked at a threshold while that ram's animation
  plays (about 1.3s), then continues.
- damageLevel = how many thresholds `shown` has passed (0 to 3). When it goes up, play
  that ram once. When it goes down (desktop scrolling back), fade the cracks out in
  300ms with no bull animation. If several thresholds are jumped at once, play only the
  highest ram and set the damage to match.
- Do NOT keep progress in React state. Keep it in refs and update the DOM with GSAP, so
  there are no re-renders per frame.
